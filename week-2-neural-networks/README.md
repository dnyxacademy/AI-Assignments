# Week 2 — Neural networks and deep learning

**9–14 November**

You build a network by hand in NumPy before touching a framework. It is the hardest day of the week and the one that makes everything afterwards make sense. Then PyTorch, transfer learning, and the embeddings that Week 3 is built on.

## What you will learn this week

- The neuron, loss, gradient descent, backpropagation
- PyTorch: tensors, autograd, the training loop, the GPU
- Convolutional networks and transfer learning
- Embeddings, attention, and why transformers won
- Dropout, weight decay, early stopping, and tracking experiments

## Read this before you start

Monday will be frustrating, and that is the assignment working rather than failing. Do not skip to PyTorch — you only have to write backpropagation by hand once in your life, and doing it is the difference between using a framework and understanding one. Also: assignment 4 on embeddings is the direct foundation of Week 3's retrieval work, so do not treat it as the easy Thursday.

## The AI strand this week
