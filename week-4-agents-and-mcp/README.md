# Week 4 — Agents, MCP and shipping

**23–28 November**

From something that answers to something that acts. Tool calling, guardrails, traces, an MCP server of your own, and then the whole thing deployed with an honest account of what it gets wrong.

## What you will learn this week

- Think, act, observe — and when a plain function is the better answer
- Planning, reflection and routing; guardrails, cost control and tracing
- MCP: hosts, clients, servers; writing your own in Python
- FastAPI, rate limits, logging, and deploying
- Documenting results and failures honestly

## Read this before you start

Two specific ones. **Cap your agent's loops and its budget** before you let it run unattended — an agent with no iteration limit calling a paid model is the fastest way to an unpleasant bill, and it happens to someone every batch.

And **do not delete your earlier work while tidying up.** The final project requires your Week 1 to 3 work to still be visible: the baseline, the deep-learning comparison, the retrieval evaluation. The progression is what is being assessed.
